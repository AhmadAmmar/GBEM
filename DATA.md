# Data used by this project

No input data, derived building-level data or bibliographic exports are stored in this repository. The scripts expect users to obtain the data themselves under the relevant terms.

| Data | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Energy Performance Certificates (England and Wales) | Department for Energy Security and Net Zero, EPC open data service | Registration required; follow the service's licence and address-data restrictions. Building-level extracts and maps must not be committed. |
| Energy Performance Certificates (Northern Ireland) | Department of Finance, Northern Ireland | Access and reuse under the provider's terms. |
| Building footprints and addresses (UPRN) | Ordnance Survey, OpenStreetMap | OS products under OS licence terms; OpenStreetMap under ODbL. |
| Sentinel-2, Landsat, other satellite data | Copernicus, USGS, Google Earth Engine | Under the respective data policies. |
| LiDAR | Environment Agency National LiDAR Programme | Open Government Licence. |
| Literature database exports (review) | Scopus, Web of Science | Licensed databases; exports are not redistributed. |

Outputs that identify individual properties (for example building-level predicted ratings with coordinates) are treated as restricted and are excluded by `.gitignore`.
