# GBEM Atlas (public)

Interactive atlas of domestic energy performance in Greater London: grid cells, neighbourhoods (LSOA 2021), streets
and urban form types, with rankings, trends, retrofit needs and model results.

Open it at https://ahmadammar.github.io/GBEM/

- Built with `viz/atlas.py --areas london --public` from the main branch of this repository.
- Aggregates only: every grid cell, neighbourhood and street shown has at least 10 certificates. There is no
  building-level layer.
- For research and communication. It is not a source of information about individual homes.

Data: Energy Performance Certificates for England and Wales (MHCLG), boundaries (ONS), deprivation (MHCLG), energy
consumption (DESNZ): contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Contains OS
